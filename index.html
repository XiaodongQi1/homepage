About me

I am a Research Fellow at the Centre in Computational Technologies for Finance, Nanyang Technological University (CCTF–NTU), Singapore, under the supervision of Prof. Yonggang Wen and Prof. Yi Li.

Research interests

My research focuses on scalable and trustworthy distributed data systems, including parallel smart contract execution, blockchain sharding, Byzantine-resilient storage, and verifiable, privacy-preserving AI.

I am also interested in AI agent memory management, particularly efficient storage and retrieval of long-term memory, memory consolidation and updating, and consistent memory sharing across agents.
